- The `csrf_token` cookie is now bound to `taos_session`: it is minted for the new session at login, re-issued whenever it does not verify for the request's session, and `verify_csrf` rejects a cookie minted for another session (or planted from a sibling subdomain) with 403 `CSRF token not bound to session`. Pre-change unbound cookies are re-issued on the next request, so an open tab recovers after one retry (CSRF binding 2/3).
